Chocolate Cake

This chocolate cake is soft, moist, and delicious. It is perfect for birthdays and special occasions.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix the flour, sugar, cocoa powder, and baking powder in a bowl.
  3. Add the eggs, milk, and butter. Mix everything well.
  4. Pour the batter into a greased cake pan.
  5. Bake for 30 minutes.
  6. Let the cake cool before serving. Serve it carefully.
